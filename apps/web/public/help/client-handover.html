Scan showroom items into a customer intent

Use the phone camera while walking the showroom with a customer. Each active MP label resolves to its governed Product Master item before it is added to the lead or intent.

Customer + lead details→Scan showroom item→Verify product row→Set room / quantity→Save intent / create quote

Worked example - training data only

Training example: while showing a customer the master bathroom, scan the display label for Calacatta Ivory 1200 x 600. Confirm the returned warehouse/display code and image, set Room / use to "Master bathroom wall", enter the required area and wastage, then scan the next selected item. Create the lead with intent; later scans on the editable Intent page must be saved or submitted before leaving.

How to complete it

  1. On mobile, open Leads -> New lead and select the customer, project, source and next action.
  2. In Product intent, tap Scan showroom item, then Scan with camera. Allow camera access for the Marble Park HTTPS site when the phone asks.
  3. Hold the MP QR inside the frame. A valid active product, lot or display label adds its linked Product Master item; an inactive or unknown label changes nothing.
  4. Verify the image, permanent warehouse/display code, design, size, finish and rate shown on the new row. A display asset is non-sellable, but its linked Product Master variant is the commercial item added to intent.
  5. Set Room / use and quantity. For area-priced tiles, enter required area and wastage; the system derives physical packs.
  6. Scan more items as the customer walks the showroom. Search by code remains available when the camera is blocked or a label is damaged.
  7. Choose Create lead + intent. On an existing editable intent, use Scan showroom item, review the new row, then Save draft or Save & submit so the scan persists.
  8. From Labels & Scan, a successful scan can also open Catalogue, preload Add to intent, or start Quick quote; always confirm customer and pricing before saving.

Before you finish

✓ Sales users need only an authenticated account and their normal lead/intent permission; label printing permission is not required to scan.

✓ The camera starts only after a deliberate tap and stops after a successful read.

✓ The encoded QR value is never repeated on screen; the printed label code is the fallback.

✓ Scanning does not reserve, issue or increase stock.

✓ An existing intent must remain editable; submitted, quoted or cancelled intents are frozen for audit.

✓ Do not leave the page until the scanned row has been saved or submitted.

Set up master data and products

Create controlled brands, categories and generic SKUs; for tiles, separate design content, governed geometry and inwardable variants before any transaction uses them.

Design registry→Size geometry→Variant SKU→Opening stock / GRN→Lot / display asset

Worked example - training data only

Training example: create design "Calacatta Ivory" once with its images, marble-look material, ivory colour, polished finish and wall/floor usage. Create Size Master "1200 x 600 mm" with calculated area. Then create the inwardable variant that combines that design, size and finish; the system issues its permanent warehouse SKU. Add supplier or old showroom codes as aliases, never by renaming the SKU.

How to complete it

  1. Create brand, category, finish, UOM, tax and tile-size masters first.
  2. In Tile Workspace, create the design once with brand, collection, material/type, surface/finish, colour, pattern, usage, description and images.
  3. Create each sellable design × size × finish variant; review pieces per box, UOM, loose-piece policy, pricing and the permanent warehouse SKU.
  4. Add supplier, legacy, barcode or showroom codes as aliases instead of renaming the warehouse SKU.
  5. Download a fresh Excel template so bulk-import dropdowns match live master data; correct every validation failure before confirming.
  6. Record opening stock or a PO/manual GRN against the variant to create exact lots; for tiles enter boxes plus optional loose pieces, batch, shade, caliber and grade.
  7. Register a display as a separate asset. Choose a source lot only when physical stock is consumed, then print the display label.

Before you finish

✓ Design and size are reusable catalogue masters; Product-backed variant is the only stock identity.

✓ Warehouse SKU and design code are immutable after creation.

✓ An imported variant starts with zero stock.

✓ Display stock changes only through issue-to-display or return-to-lot actions.

✓ Stock exists only after a posted opening session or GRN.

Inventory · Office · Owner · 13 min

Procure shortages and receive stock

Turn open purchase demand into purchase orders, receive partial GRNs, and create traceable lots without breaking the customer order link.

Overview→Demand→Purchase orders→PO / manual receiving→History / allocation

Worked example - training data only

Training example: receive 10 boxes of Calacatta Ivory 1200 x 600, 3 pieces per box, plus 1 permitted loose piece. The GRN posts 31 base pieces into one exact lot with supplier batch, shade, caliber, grade, unit cost and location. Labels are created from that posted GRN; the label job does not post the stock again.

How to complete it

  1. Start on Procurement Overview. Its KPI cards and schedule read the live demand, PO and GRN registers; purchase commitment value includes only POs with a captured commercial value and identifies missing costs instead of treating them as real zero spend.
  2. Open Demand for customer shortages or Purchase Orders for planned replenishment. Search, filter and sort the server-paged queue; select only active demand and choose a governed supplier.
  3. For a planned PO, search the Product/variant code and enter quantity. Tile lines accept boxes plus allowed loose pieces and snapshot the pack conversion.
  4. Record expected date, unit cost, discount, GST and notes, then download the supplier PO PDF.
  5. In Receiving, choose the PO and enter only quantities physically delivered; every receipt line starts blank and never defaults to the outstanding balance.
  6. Capture supplier challan/bill, stock location, damaged pieces, batch and tile shade/caliber/grade. Use Manual GRN only for a real delivery without a prior PO and record the reason.
  7. After posting, open History and use Create labels on the GRN. It hands the exact accepted lines and lots to the Physical Identity Desk without reposting stock.
  8. Review exact lots, Pending Inward allocation and stock reconciliation. The form clears after success.

Before you finish

✓ Never add live stock directly to balances.

✓ Received quantity cannot exceed the remaining PO base quantity.

✓ Boxes and loose pieces convert to base pieces with an immutable snapshot.

✓ Damaged quantity is isolated from available stock.

✓ Every inward retains supplier, PO/manual reason, GRN, variant, lot, location, actor and source links.

Inventory · Dispatch · 9 min

Print labels, scan and manage lots

Create auditable labels from recent GRNs, exact lots, products or display assets; print only the chosen labels and verify the real branded QR payload before movement.

Posted GRN / display→Select exact subject→Create unique labels→Print and confirm→Scan and trace

Worked example - training data only

Training example: open the recent GRN for Calacatta Ivory, select its exact lot and create 10 carton labels. Prepare a run for only labels 1-10, print on the chosen physical template, and confirm only after paper is produced. A salesperson may later scan any active label to open Catalogue or add the linked variant to intent; no stock changes during scan or print.

How to complete it

  1. After posting a PO or manual GRN, use Create labels in Procurement History or open Recent GRNs in the Physical Identity Desk.
  2. Select accepted receipt lines and verify the product image, permanent code, exact lot, location, accepted quantity and pieces per pack.
  3. Confirm the number of physical packs or items that need unique labels. The suggestion is derived from accepted pieces and pack size but remains an explicit operator decision.
  4. For a shelf, existing lot, carton or showroom asset, use Other subjects and search the authoritative Product Master, lot or Display Asset register.
  5. Open Print & reprint, select the exact unique labels, physical paper/template and reason. A preview creates an audited prepared run but does not mark anything printed.
  6. In the isolated print screen, confirm only after the printer produces the labels. Cancel or failed leaves print counts unchanged.
  7. In Scan & verify, tap Scan with camera on mobile or use a handheld scanner/manual code. Camera access starts only after the tap and stops after a successful read.
  8. Review the full product, lot, batch, location or display identity. Open Catalogue for detail, Add to intent for guided lead intake, or Quick quote to preload Quote Studio; customer and pricing checks are still required.
  9. Void a damaged or superseded physical label with a reason; create a new print run for every reprint.

Before you finish

✓ Creating or printing labels never posts stock; only a GRN, opening session or governed movement changes inventory.

✓ Every QR encodes MP-LABEL:<unique label code>; the MP centre mark is branding and the human-readable code remains the fallback.

✓ Reprints retain the same physical identity and add print audit history.

✓ A display label identifies a non-sellable showroom asset and must never be treated as available stock.

✓ Vendor-provided displays create no stock movement; displays issued from a received lot post an audited lot issue and can return only to that original lot.

Inventory · Owner · 7 min

Move stock to showroom display

Create one non-sellable display identity from an exact received lot, or register a genuine free vendor sample without changing stock.

GRN or vendor sample→Exact product and lot→Display code and position→Audited issue / register→QR label and lifecycle

Worked example - training data only

Training example A: issue 1 piece from the Calacatta Ivory GRN lot to display code WALL-A-014 in zone Tile Gallery, position Bay A. Available stock reduces by 1 and the display remains linked to the original lot. Training example B: register a free vendor sample with its own display code and position; no GRN, lot or stock quantity is created.

How to complete it

  1. For anything purchased or intended as saleable stock, post the PO/manual GRN first so the physical receipt has a governed lot.
  2. Open Display Assets and choose From inventory. Search the Product Master item, choose the exact available lot and location, enter the quantity, display code, showroom zone and position.
  3. Saving posts one display_issue out of that exact lot, creates the non-sellable display asset and prepares its QR label job.
  4. Use Vendor sample only for a free/non-stock physical sample. This registers the asset but creates no GRN and no stock movement.
  5. Print the display QR from Labels & Scan and attach it to the matching physical sample.
  6. Use Inspect and Maintenance for routine lifecycle updates. Remove with a reason; a stock-issued display can return only its complete issued quantity to the original lot.

Before you finish

✓ Purchased goods never bypass GRN.

✓ A display is always non-sellable and excluded from available stock.

✓ Every stock-issued display retains product, source lot, location, quantity, actor and ledger posting.

✓ A vendor sample never increases stock.

✓ Every lifecycle change and reprint retains audit history.

Daily control

Finish each shift with source truth intact

Completion checks

  1. Every scanned sales row is verified and saved before leaving the page.
  2. Every tile inward references the Product-backed variant and exact lot.
  3. Every display has a unique code, product, zone and position.
  4. Every reprint, void, removal or return retains its reason and audit history.
  5. Inventory Reconciliation has no critical aggregate-versus-lot mismatch.

When something does not work

Camera blocked: allow Camera for the Marble Park HTTPS site, close other camera tabs, or use the printed code.

Unknown scan: verify the full code and that label, product, lot and display are active.

Tile cannot be inwarded: complete Design, Size and Product-backed Variant first.

Never edit balances to repair a receipt, display or scan issue.
